# fun-grilling

A layer over Matt Pocock's `grilling` that makes a session engaging without making it lighter:

- It opens with a map of the design tree.
- Rounds go through `AskUserQuestion`, with previews of the consequences.
- After every round comes a payoff: what was settled, unlocked and ruled out, and what's left.
- Question formats vary: pick the mockup, rank the stakes, bet then reveal, pre-mortem, steelman.
- There are playbacks and painless pause points, and it closes with a decision card.

It fires alongside `grilling` (and so on wayfinder grilling tickets). Its rules come from
[`references/evidence.md`](references/evidence.md).
